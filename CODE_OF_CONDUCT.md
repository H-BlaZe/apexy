# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Apexy to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst
- Own your mistakes and learn from them

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/H-BlaZe) or by opening a confidential [security advisory](https://github.com/H-BlaZe/Apexy/security/advisories/new). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously.
